# Design Direction: BGMI Breakdown

## Identity & Voice
- **Product**: BGMI Breakdown — Competitive Intelligence & Tactical Handbook for Battlegrounds Mobile India.
- **Tone**: Tactical, authoritative, focused, and precise. Reads like an esports briefing room and tactical field manual, not a generic marketing landing page.
- **Audience**: Competitive BGMI gamers, tournament viewers, squad leaders, and rank pushers.

## Dials
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)** — Strong typographic hierarchy and high-contrast telemetry; avoids garish neon glows or sterile grey voids.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Structured with Intentional Breaks)** — Consistent grid rhythm anchored by functional data tables, weapon dossiers, and player profile panels.
- **MOTION: 1 (Tactile & Purposeful)** — Fast, responsive transitions on hover and tab change. No floating elements, no looping pulsing box-shadows.

## Color System
- **Neutral Ground**: Carbon `#08090d`, Slate `#0f121a`, Surface `#161a24`
- **Border & Dividers**: Subtle tactical border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Text**: Primary `#f8fafc`, Secondary `#94a3b8`, Muted `#64748b`
- **Single Deliberate Accent**: Battlegrounds Amber `#f97316` (used exclusively for active selection, critical weapon damage, and primary callouts)
- **Functional Status Colors**: Success `#22c55e`, Caution `#eab308`, High Risk `#ef4444` (strictly applied to in-game statistics like zone danger, loot tier, and win rates)

## Typography & Details
- **Headings**: Heavy, clean, condensed sans-serif with tight tracking.
- **Data Readouts**: Clean monospace strictly for numbers, coordinates, timers, and weapon specs.
- **Zero Decorative Emojis**: Removed from headers, badges, and body copy.
- **Zero Em Dashes**: Strict adherence to R-02 (use colons, commas, or parentheses instead).
- **No Background Dot Grids or Radial Glow Orbs**: Strict adherence to R-01 and R-07.
